ABOUT CATQUEST

Learn More About Your Feline Friends

CatQuest is an informative website created to help cat lovers and owners learn more about different cat breeds.

The website provides information about each breed's history, physical characteristics, coat and grooming needs, temperament, health, and care considerations.

Our goal is to make cat breed information easier to understand and access in one place.

Why CatQuest?

Every cat breed has its own history, characteristics, personality, and needs.

OUR PURPOSE

What CatQuest Aims to Do

Reliable Information

Provide reliable information about different cat breeds using trusted references.

Easy to Understand

Present breed information in a clear and organized way that is easy to understand.

Better Understanding

Help users understand the characteristics and needs of different cat breeds.

Responsible Care

Help current and potential cat owners become more informed about cat care.
